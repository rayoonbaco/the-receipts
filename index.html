GLOBAL AI HACKATHON · CARNEGIE MELLON HUB

From a challenge brief
to a working answer.

Housing Navigator is my solo submission for the RealPage challenge at Hack-Nation’s 7th Global AI Hackathon, October 3–4, 2026.

The problem

A resident needs to understand which housing rules may apply to their home. The answer depends on location, dates and property facts—and a confident summary alone is not enough.

What I built

I directed the research, AI-assisted implementation, evaluation and deployment of a source-to-answer workflow. Select a supplied address and date, explore six everyday questions, then open Check my homework to inspect the supporting passages and unresolved facts.

The evidence

The documented release contains 310 rules and 500 addresses across four dates. The repository records 72 offline engine tests and preserves failures, repairs, source audits and coverage gaps.

Prototype scope: The demo serves saved challenge results. It is not legal advice, continuous monitoring or nationwide coverage. Missing facts remain unresolved; real-resident testing and a legally reviewed pilot are next steps.
